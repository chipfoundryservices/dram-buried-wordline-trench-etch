# Glossary

**6F² / 4F²** — Cell area in units of the squared minimum half-pitch F.

**Active area** — Silicon region that holds transistors, bounded by STI.

**ARDE** — Aspect-ratio-dependent etching: etch rate that changes with depth-to-width ratio.

**Aspect ratio (AR)** — Trench depth divided by width.

**BCAT / buried word line (BWL)** — Access transistor whose gate conductor lies entirely within a trench below the silicon surface.

**Bowing** — Outward bulge of a sidewall below the mask.

**CD-SAXS** — Critical-dimension small-angle X-ray scattering.

**Crossed-strings rule** — Exact method for 2-D view factors between diffuse surfaces.

**Divot** — Recess at the STI liner where the trench etch removes liner faster than its neighbours.

**Fin height** — Depth of the STI floor below the silicon floor in a word-line trench.

**Free-molecular (Knudsen) flow** — Flow in which molecules collide with walls far more often than with each other.

**GIDL** — Gate-induced drain leakage.

**Hard mask** — Patterned inorganic or carbon film that withstands the etch.

**Ion angular distribution** — Spread of ion arrival angles at the wafer; σ in this book.

**Ion-limited / neutral-limited** — Regimes of the synergy rate law in which one supply term dominates.

**Metal recess** — Etch-back of the word-line conductor below the silicon surface.

**OCD** — Optical critical-dimension metrology (scatterometry).

**Passing word line** — Word line that crosses STI next to the end of an active area.

**Passivation** — Protective film on the sidewall formed from etch products and added gases.

**Pitch walking** — Alternating space widths left by self-aligned multiple patterning.

**RCAT / S-RCAT** — Recess-channel and saddle (sphere-shaped) recess-channel array transistors.

**Retention time** — Time a DRAM cell holds its state without refresh.

**Row hammer** — Disturbance of cells by repeated activation of a neighbouring word line.

**Saddle fin** — Silicon left standing above the deeper STI floor, wrapped by the gate.

**Sticking coefficient (s)** — Probability that a neutral is lost at a wall collision.

**STI** — Shallow-trench isolation.

**t₀** — Time to etch one trench width of silicon in open area.
