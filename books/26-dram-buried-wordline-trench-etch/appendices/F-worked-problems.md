# Appendix F — Worked Problems

**F.1 Line of sight.** A 18 nm trench is 144 nm deep. What fraction of a fully reactive radical reaches the floor directly?
*AR = 8; W = √65 − 8 = 0.062. About 6 %.*

**F.2 Neutral starvation.** Using Appendix B, by what factor does the floor flux of a radical with s = 0.05 fall between AR = 2 and AR = 8?
*0.501 → 0.176, a factor of 2.8.*

**F.3 Ion persistence.** For σ = 4°, by what factor does ion floor flux fall over the same range?
*0.889 → 0.570, a factor of 1.6. The floor's ion-to-neutral ratio rises by 2.8/1.6 ≈ 1.8.*

**F.4 Fin-height sensitivity.** From the Chapter 7 table, estimate the change in fin height if the etch stops at 14 t₀ instead of 15 t₀.
*20.3 nm versus 22.3 nm: 2 nm shorter. Silicon depth changes by 5.4 nm.*

**F.5 Real time.** With w = 20 nm and an open-area silicon rate of 240 nm/min, how long is 15 t₀?
*t₀ = 20/240 min = 5 s; 15 t₀ = 75 s.*

**F.6 Pitch walking.** Two alternate trenches are 19 nm and 21 nm wide. At equal etch time, which has the taller fin, and why?
*The narrower one: it reaches a given aspect ratio at a shallower depth, so its silicon slows earlier while its oxide keeps going (Chapter 7).*

**F.7 Effective selectivity.** Open-area silicon-to-carbon selectivity is 20. The silicon averages 0.43 R₀ over the etch, while carbon erodes at a constant rate. What is the effective selectivity?
*20 × 0.43 ≈ 8.6.*
