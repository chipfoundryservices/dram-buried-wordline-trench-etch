# Appendix G — Further Reading

The following are foundational references the book builds on. Citation details are given as remembered; verify against the original before citing formally.

- J. W. Coburn and H. F. Winters, "Ion- and electron-assisted gas-surface chemistry — an important effect in plasma etching," *Journal of Applied Physics* 50, 3189 (1979).
- R. A. Gottscho, C. W. Jurgensen and D. J. Vitkavage, "Microscopic uniformity in plasma etching," *Journal of Vacuum Science & Technology B* 10, 2133 (1992).
- P. Clausing, "Über die Strömung sehr verdünnter Gase durch Röhren von beliebiger Länge," *Annalen der Physik* 12, 961 (1932).
- H. C. Hottel and A. F. Sarofim, *Radiative Transfer*, McGraw-Hill (1967). Crossed-strings method.
- J. Y. Kim et al., "The breakthrough in data retention time of DRAM using recess-channel-array transistor (RCAT) for 88 nm feature size and beyond," VLSI Technology Symposium (2003).
- J. Y. Kim et al., "S-RCAT (sphere-shaped-recess-channel-array transistor) technology for 70 nm DRAM feature size and beyond," VLSI Technology Symposium (2005).
- T. Schloesser et al., "A 6F² buried wordline DRAM cell for 40 nm and beyond," IEDM (2008).
- M. A. Lieberman and A. J. Lichtenberg, *Principles of Plasma Discharges and Materials Processing*, 2nd ed., Wiley (2005).
- V. M. Donnelly and A. Kornblit, "Plasma etching: yesterday, today, and tomorrow," *Journal of Vacuum Science & Technology A* 31, 050825 (2013).
- K. J. Kanarik et al., "Overview of atomic layer etching in the semiconductor industry," *Journal of Vacuum Science & Technology A* 33, 020802 (2015).
- Y. Kim et al., "Flipping bits in memory without accessing them: an experimental study of DRAM disturbance errors," ISCA (2014). Row hammer.
