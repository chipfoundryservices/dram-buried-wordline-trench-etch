# Appendix C — Constants, Units and Conversions

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Boltzmann constant k | 1.381 × 10⁻²³ J/K = 8.617 × 10⁻⁵ eV/K |
| Elementary charge e | 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C |
| 1 mTorr | 0.1333 Pa |
| Thermal speed (mean) | v̄ = √(8kT/πm) |
| Flux on a surface | Γ = n v̄ / 4 |
| Mean free path | λ = kT / (√2 π d² p) |

**Representative values.** At 10 mTorr and 300 K, the mean free path of a Br atom is around 5 mm, so the Knudsen number for a 20 nm trench exceeds 10⁵. The mean thermal speed of Br at 300 K is about 280 m/s.

**Units in this book.** Lengths in the model are normalised by trench width w; time by t₀ = w/R₀(Si). With w = 20 nm and R₀ = 200 nm/min, t₀ = 6 s, so the Chapter 7 etch to t = 15 t₀ corresponds to about 90 s of main etch.
